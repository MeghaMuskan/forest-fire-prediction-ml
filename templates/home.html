What is the Fire Weather Index?

The Fire Weather Index (FWI) is an internationally recognized numerical rating used to estimate wildfire danger. It synthesizes temperature, relative humidity, wind speed, and rainfall — along with fuel-moisture codes (FFMC, DMC) and spread-rate indicators (ISI) — into a single score representing potential fire intensity.
